# Figure Captions

## sentence_environment_uncertainty.png

Action confidence and state-belief uncertainty measured within fixed-state reasoning traces and across executed environment steps. Sentence-prefix curves use 7,084 prefixes from 46 matched states. Environment-step curves use 1,276 consecutive states from 95 trajectories. State-belief uncertainty is mean Shannon entropy over wall-left, wall-right, wall-up, wall-down, key-held, and door-open candidate probabilities at temperature 0.7. The panels use different populations and progress definitions.
